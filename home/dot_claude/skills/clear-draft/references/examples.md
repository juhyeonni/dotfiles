# Real exemplars and Before / After passages

Part 1 is **published text, unedited**. Part 2 applies those principles as corrections.

# Part 1. Published papers

## 1-1. Watson & Crick (1953) — the textbook case of conclusion-first

> "WE wish to suggest a structure for the salt of deoxyribose nucleic acid (D.N.A.). This structure has novel features which are of considerable biological interest."

> "It has not escaped our notice that the specific pairing we have postulated immediately suggests a possible copying mechanism for the genetic material."

*Nature* 171(4356), 737–738 (1953).

**What to take from it**
- The first sentence *is* the paper. Not one line of background precedes it.
- The strength of the claim is calibrated exactly. `suggest`, `possible` — because that is as far as the evidence went. Not an overused hedge but a **precise** one.
- The largest implication sits at **the end of the sentence, the stress position** (Gopen & Swan, principle 2).

## 1-2. Vaswani et al. (2017), "Attention Is All You Need" — the full abstract

> "The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks in an encoder-decoder configuration. The best performing models also connect the encoder and decoder through an attention mechanism. We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely. Experiments on two machine translation tasks show these models to be superior in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train. Our model achieves 28.4 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task, improving over the existing best results, including ensembles by over 2 BLEU. …"

arXiv:1706.03762.

**What to take from it**
- Sentences 1–2 = the existing approach (old information) → sentence 3 = ours (new information). Gopen & Swan's old-to-new flow exactly.
- That third sentence alone carries **what (the Transformer) / on what basis (attention only) / what was discarded (recurrence, convolutions)**.
- Active voice, `We propose`. The subject is not hidden.
- A number (28.4 BLEU) follows the claim immediately. No adjective goes unsupported.

## 1-3. Polack et al. (2020), NEJM — the standard for a structured abstract

> **Background** … Safe and effective vaccines are needed urgently.
> **Methods** In an ongoing multinational, placebo-controlled, observer-blinded, pivotal efficacy trial, we randomly assigned persons 16 years of age or older in a 1:1 ratio to receive two doses, 21 days apart, of either placebo or the BNT162b2 vaccine candidate (30 μg per dose). …
> **Results** A total of 43,548 participants underwent randomization … BNT162b2 was 95% effective in preventing Covid-19 (95% credible interval, 90.3 to 97.6). …
> **Conclusions** A two-dose regimen of BNT162b2 conferred 95% protection against Covid-19 in persons 16 years of age or older. …

*N Engl J Med* 383(27), 2603–2615 (2020).

**What to take from it**
- The stacked modifiers in Methods (`multinational, placebo-controlled, observer-blinded, pivotal`) are long but **every one is needed to judge the study**. Nothing is decorative.
- Every number carries an uncertainty interval. The hedging is expressed **in numbers, not in words**.
- The Conclusions never exceed the Results. Even the observation window (median 2 months) is stated plainly.

## 1-4. Shannon (1948) — how to define a problem

> "The fundamental problem of communication is that of reproducing at one point either exactly or approximately a message selected at another point. Frequently the messages have meaning; that is they refer to or are correlated according to some system with certain physical or conceptual entities. **These semantic aspects of communication are irrelevant to the engineering problem.** The significant aspect is that the actual message is one selected from a set of possible messages."

*Bell System Technical Journal* 27 (1948).

**What to take from it**
- **A non-goal nailed down in a single sentence.** That one line stops every later discussion from leaking. It does the same job as the "범위 밖" item in a GitHub issue or a design doc.
- The very next sentence moves straight to "then what does matter". It does not only discard.

## 1-5. Japanese — the form that puts 主題文 in the first sentence

> 本研究の目的は，高校の情報科と生物科を横断した探究型授業を対象として，課題の設定を支援することで学際的思考の育成を目指した教育実践の開発と評価をすることである．具体的には，生徒の研究スキル，学際的思考，および探究に対する認識はどの程度変容するか，課題の設定の違いが学際的思考にどのような影響を与えるのかを明らかにする．

正司豪・石橋希・尾澤重知,『日本教育工学会論文誌』49(4), 701–717 (2025).

**What to take from it**
- Opens with 「本研究の目的は…ことである．」, fixing the purpose first (重点先行).
- 「具体的には」 marks the move from abstract to concrete explicitly.
- 「」 brackets the operational concepts, closing off ambiguity.

## 1-6. Japanese — background → gap in prior work → this study

> 受験は，子ども達やその家族にとって大きなライフイベントである。中学受験を「試練」ととらえ成長を感じる者もいる一方，年齢に見合わない勉強量や親の過干渉により，中学受験を否定的にとらえる者も一定数いる。受験を巡る言説は散見されるが，実証的研究は極めて少なく，中学受験を取り上げた心理学的な研究はほとんどない。本研究は，中学受験を経験した当事者が，社会に出た後に自らの経験をどのように意味づけしているのかに関し，25―39歳の男女536名の回答を分析した。

大橋恵・井梅由美子・藤後悦子,『教育心理学研究』74(2), 65–79 (2026).

**What to take from it**
- 「が」 is used **only adversatively** (「散見されるが」). Overusing it as a plain connective blurs the logic in Japanese.
- 一文一義 is held from start to finish.
- The gap is narrowed in two stages, 「極めて少なく」 then 「ほとんどない」.

## 1-7. Korean — definition → core → pivot

> 공유의사결정(shared decision-making, SDM)은 의료인이 해당 임상 상황에서 가능한 선택지와 각 선택지의 이익, 위험 및 불확실성을 설명하고, 환자가 자신의 선호와 가치, 생활 여건, 감당할 수 있는 부담을 밝히며, 양측이 이를 바탕으로 숙의를 거쳐 결정을 함께 형성해 가는 과정이자 실천 체계이다. SDM의 핵심은 환자가 우선시하는 가치, 유지하고자 하는 일상과 기능, 감수 가능한 부담, 수용하기 어려운 결과를 의료 선택의 중심에 놓는 데 있다. …
>
> 그러나 한국의 의료 결정, 특히 중증질환에서의 의사결정은 환자-의료인 양자 관계만으로 설명되기 어렵다. …

유상호,「한국 의료에서 공유의사결정과 가족」, 『대한의사협회지』 69(7), 577–585 (2026).

**What to take from it**
- The first sentence is long, but its parallel structure is uniform — `A하고, B하며, 양측이 C하는 과정` — so it stays readable. Length is not the problem; length without structure is.
- The abbreviation is defined with the original term on first use, then only SDM is used consistently.
- The paragraph pivot is marked by a single word, 「그러나」, and the counter-proposition follows immediately.
- No translationese (`~에 의해`, `가지다`, `되어지다`).

## 1-8. Control case — a Korean abstract with real room to tighten

> 본 연구결과를 통해 간호대학생이 앞으로 임상 현장에서 경험하게 되는 다양한 스트레스 상황을 적절한 대처하고 적응하면서 전문직 간호역량을 높이기 위해 대학교육에서부터 자아존중감과 자기효능감을 증진시키려는 노력이 필요하다 여겨지며, 자아존중감과 자기효능감을 활용한 자아탄력성 향상 프로그램 개발과 적용이 요구된다.

이선영·이정숙·김윤영,『디지털융복합연구』15(5), 401–409 (2017).

**Diagnosis** — the high-frequency problem cases from 한재영 (2015) appear verbatim.

- `본 연구결과를 통해` → translationese
- `~여겨지며`, `~이 요구된다` → agentless passive
- `적절한 대처하고` → subject–predicate agreement error
- Over 130 characters in one sentence, carrying two points (education is needed + a program should be built)

**Correction**

> 이 결과는 대학 교육 단계에서 자아존중감과 자기효능감을 함께 높여야 함을 보여준다. 두 요인을 활용한 자아탄력성 향상 프로그램을 개발하고 현장에 적용할 것을 제안한다.

# Part 2. Before / After corrections

## 2-1. Korean — translationese and passives

| # | Before | After | Principle applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 본 연구에서는 A에 대한 분석이 수행되었다. | 이 연구는 A를 분석했다. | Drop the passive, drop `~에 대한` |
| 2 | 해당 기능은 사용자의 데이터의 처리의 지연을 야기시킬 가능성을 가진다. | 이 기능은 사용자 데이터 처리를 지연시킬 수 있다. | Break the `의` chain, drop `가지다`, clean up `시키다` |
| 3 | 이러한 결과를 통해 볼 때, 유의미한 시사점이 도출되어졌다고 할 수 있을 것이다. | 이 결과는 X가 Y에 영향을 준다는 것을 보여준다. | Remove the double passive and the academic cliché, supply actual content |
| 4 | 배포에 있어서 문제가 발생되어 확인이 필요한 상황입니다. | 배포가 실패했습니다. 원인 확인이 필요합니다. | Drop `~에 있어서` (において), one point per sentence |
| 5 | 성능 개선이 이루어질 수 있도록 최적화를 진행하였습니다. | 쿼리를 최적화해 응답 시간을 320ms에서 90ms로 줄였습니다. | Undo the nominalization, add the supporting number |

## 2-2. English — applying Gopen & Swan

**(a) Subject separated from its verb**

> ✗ The smallest of the URF's (URFA6L), a 207-nucleotide reading frame overlapping out of phase the NH2-terminal portion of the adenosinetriphosphatase subunit 6 gene, **has been identified** as …
>
> ✓ The smallest of the URF's **is** URFA6L, a 207-nucleotide reading frame … **It has been identified** as …

23 words between subject and verb, split into two sentences. (Gopen & Swan 1990)

**(b) Stress position**

> ✗ A 40% reduction in latency was observed in our experiments, which we ran on eight GPUs.
>
> ✓ In experiments on eight GPUs, our method **reduced latency by 40%**.

The information to be remembered moved to the end of the sentence.

**(c) Nominalization → verb**

> ✗ An evaluation of the performance of the system was carried out by the team.
>
> ✓ We evaluated the system's performance.
>
> ✗ The implementation of the proposed modification resulted in an improvement of throughput.
>
> ✓ The proposed change improved throughput.

EASE: "Do not overuse passive constructions." / digital.gov: bring the hidden verb (`-tion`) back to life.

**(d) Over-hedging**

> ✗ It could potentially be suggested that the results may possibly indicate a somewhat improved outcome under certain conditions.
>
> ✓ The results indicate a 12% improvement (95% CI, 7–17) under condition A. Whether this holds under condition B remains untested.

Do not blur uncertainty with words; state it **as an interval and an untested region**. (Ryba et al. 2021)

**(e) Academic clichés**

> ✗ These novel findings robustly elucidate a paradigm that underscores the critical importance of the mechanism.
>
> ✓ These findings show that the mechanism controls X.

General scientific jargon is the main driver of 130 years of declining readability. (Plavén-Sigray et al. 2017)

## 2-3. Japanese — applying the 文化庁 standard

| # | Before | After | Principle applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 本システムにおいては，データの処理の遅延が発生することが確認された。 | 本システムでは，データ処理が遅延した。 | 「の」連続回避, 受身抑制, 一文短縮 |
